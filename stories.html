MENTORSHIP CLIENT · FIRST SESSION REFLECTION

“That sense of accountability was ground-breaking.”

Reflecting on our first session together, I must say it was a lot of inner searching — realising myself more, if that makes sense. When I spoke it out, it made it more realistic, especially my goals and weaknesses. What was profound for me was the sense of “I need to do what I set out to do” in that session, because I am now held accountable. That sense of accountability was ground-breaking, to say the least.

My expectation: I expect to grow in wisdom in areas like my finances, starting and running my business, and being proactive. I do not want to stay the same — I want to be stronger, wiser and more reliable. If I set out to do something, I should do it, not procrastinate and make excuses for myself. I want to be a successful entrepreneur who leaves a legacy behind, I want my family to be sorted, and I want to live a life of fulfilment in living out my purpose.

MENTORSHIP PARTICIPANT · YOUTH LEADER & SOCIAL MEDIA MANAGER

“Consistency and intentional choices, not perfection.”

Over the past two months, the Legacy Builders Mentorship Program has really impacted me. I’ve learned the importance of honesty — being truthful about where I am instead of pretending everything’s fine. The mentorship has given me wisdom and practical insight for building discipline in my daily life, helping me evaluate my roles as a follower of Christ, a youth leader, a social media manager, and the man I’m becoming.

One question that sticks with me is whether my daily choices are drawing me closer to who God called me to be, or pulling me away. It’s changed how I see consistency in my fitness journey too — it’s not about perfection, but consistency and intentional choices. What I appreciate most is how Pastor Chaeslin has genuinely invested in my growth, asking tough questions and creating space for honesty without judgment. I don’t take this time, wisdom and investment lightly.

APHIWE DINGA · LIFE COACHING CLIENT

A life-changing journey with Pastor Chaeslin.

I had the incredible opportunity to work with Pastor Chaeslin Trollip as my life coach, and I am truly grateful for the transformative experience I had. His guidance, wisdom and unwavering support have had a profound impact on my life. From our very first session, his warm and compassionate demeanour created a safe space for me to open up and explore my deepest desires, dreams and challenges. His active listening skills and genuine empathy allowed me to feel heard and understood, fostering a strong sense of trust and connection.

What sets Pastor Chaeslin apart is his ability to combine spiritual guidance with practical action steps. He not only helped me gain clarity on my purpose and values but also provided practical strategies to overcome obstacles and create meaningful change in my life. His guidance was always uplifting and filled with hope, inspiring me to step out of my comfort zone and pursue my dreams fearlessly. His consistent encouragement and unwavering belief in my potential have been instrumental in boosting my confidence and self-belief — celebrating my victories, no matter how small, and gently guiding me through moments of doubt, reminding me of my inherent strength and resilience.

I wholeheartedly recommend Pastor Chaeslin Trollip as a life coach to anyone seeking personal transformation, clarity and a renewed sense of purpose. Working with him has been a life-changing journey, and I am forever grateful for his invaluable support.

— Aphiwe Dinga
